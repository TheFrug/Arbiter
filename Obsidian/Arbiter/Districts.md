Districts are the many city-states that make up the [[The Free and United Second Republic]]. Districts are federally recognized economic zones operated by a primary corporate partner and regulated via compliance systems. 
Each district has:
- **Declared Function** (What is produced, what is the districts "Republican Mission")
- **Operating Partner** (corporate)
- **Population Tiering**
Each district is internally divided into three tiers:
- **Inner Circle:** Ultra-wealthy, picturesque
- **Middle Zone:** Compliant/comfortable service class, only able to get by on service to corps or the government.
- **Outer Zone/Rubblebelt:** Precarious populations with blasted infrastructure that never seems to get any tax money for repairs.
